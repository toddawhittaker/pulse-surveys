# E5.1-11 — The roster enrolls learners only, and leaves past terms alone

**ID:** E5.1-11
**Branch:** `e5.1/roster-roles-and-term-end`
**Depends on:** 02
**Lane:** heavy ⚠
**Size:** S
**Security-relevant:** yes. Who holds a student enrollment decides who may
answer and who counts toward the comment threshold (SPEC §4), and the
teaching grant decides what an instructor may read (§2.1). SPEC §14.3 puts
both under line-by-line review.

## Context

The E5.1 epic-boundary reviews (lti-oidc, privacy-authz and threat-model, on
e14da4a) found two MEDIUM defects in `backend/app/services/roster_sync.py`,
which E5.1-02 changed.

1. `_Member.is_student` (about line 317) is a deny-list: every member is a
   student unless the roster lists the exact Instructor or TestUser role. A
   teaching assistant listed only as `…/membership/Instructor#TeachingAssistant`,
   a mentor or observer (`…/membership#Mentor`), a content developer or an
   administrator gets a student enrollment. They can submit, and they count as
   one of the five distinct commenters a stream needs before its raw comments
   show. Four real students plus one teaching assistant would show the four
   students' comments. ADR 0183's decision 4 ("enrollment means student") is
   therefore false.
2. The hourly sync walks every section with a stored roster address forever.
   Platforms commonly end or inactivate teacher enrollments when a course
   concludes. The next complete walk then ends the instructor's teaching grant
   on their own past-term section, so they lose their past reports, and SPEC
   §5's seal changes what it treats as "sections she teaches".

Two LOW findings in the same code ride along: a complete walk that read zero
members ends every grant and closes every enrollment on the section (about
line 1165 and `_ingest`), and the comment on `ROSTER_REQUEST_TIMEOUT` (about
line 146) says the bound is "a ceiling on one attempt" when it bounds the
connect and each wait between bytes, not the whole request. The private
`_RESOLVE_PERSON_FOR_USER` repeats `app.services.identity.person_for_user`.

## Decisions (settled)

- **A member is a student only if** the roster lists the Learner role
  (`app.lti.launch.LEARNER_ROLE_URI`) **and** lists neither the Instructor role
  nor any Instructor sub-role (`…/membership/Instructor#…`) **and** is not a
  test user. A member listed only with other roles (Mentor, ContentDeveloper,
  Administrator, a TeachingAssistant sub-role) is not a student. A member who
  stops being a student has their open enrollment closed by the path E5.1-02
  already uses for teachers and test users. Who holds the teaching *grant* is
  unchanged: the exact Instructor role, not dropped.
- **No teaching grant is ended once the section has ended**: when `today` is
  after `section.end_date` (inclusive last day), the grant-ending pass does
  nothing. On `end_date` itself it still runs. Closing enrollments is
  unchanged.
- **A walk that read zero members is treated as incomplete** for the
  enrollment-closing pass and the grant-ending pass. It writes nothing and
  ends nothing. A platform that truly empties a course is rare and recoverable;
  ending every grant on an empty answer is not.
- The timeout comment says what the bound covers; a total deadline stays with
  the already-carried entry for rehoming the transport code.
- `roster_sync` calls `identity.person_for_user` and drops its private copy.
- ADR 0183 is amended: decision 4's rule is corrected to the allow-list, and
  the past-term and empty-walk rules are recorded with their rejected
  alternatives (ending grants at term end as the platform says; treating an
  empty walk as complete). ADR 0183 also gains the timeout's rationale.

## Done when

1. A complete walk listing a member only as `Instructor#TeachingAssistant`,
   and another only as `Mentor`, gives neither a student enrollment, and closes
   an open one either held before. A member listed as Learner gets one
   (control). A member listed as Learner and `Instructor#TeachingAssistant`
   gets none.
2. A complete walk that drops the instructor the day after the section's
   `end_date` leaves their grant in place; the same walk on `end_date` ends it
   (boundary pair).
3. A complete walk that read zero members leaves every grant and every open
   enrollment in place; a walk that read one member still ends the others'
   grants (control).
4. The timeout comment is true, the private person lookup is gone, and ADR
   0183 says what the code does.

## Out of scope

Rehoming the LTI transport code (carried). Whether Canvas lists its Student
View user with the TestUser role is checked at platform certification (E13).
