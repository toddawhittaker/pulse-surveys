# E5.1-05 — Five small behaviour fixes

**ID:** E5.1-05
**Branch:** `e5.1/small-behaviour-fixes`
**Depends on:** E5.1-01, because both edit `reporting.py`
**Lane:** heavy (re-laned from light during the build: the 06:00 rule makes invariant-marked tests red, and an invariant-marked test is a heavy-lane path)
**Size:** M
**Security-relevant:** minimally. C6 decides when a week becomes readable, and
S8 turns a 500 into a refusal.

## Context

Five unrelated defects, each small:

- **C1.** Clearing a judged comment on revise answers 409
  (`backend/app/api/student.py:119`), and the client reads every 409 as
  "closed" (`frontend/src/api/student.ts:335`,
  `frontend/src/routes/student/StudentWeeklySurvey.tsx:508`). The refusal
  becomes a 422 shown inline.
- **C2.** The log line at `backend/app/services/grading.py:1136-1140` says the
  score is "not retried". The next run sends a fresh score, and the line
  should say so.
- **C4.** When editing a set, `ComparisonSetForm.tsx:272` does not offer the
  set's own stored length if no section runs it.
- **C6.** A week is readable as soon as it closes
  (`backend/app/services/reporting.py:990`, `:1017`), before its summary
  exists. The owner ruled on 2026-10-03 that a week's report opens at 06:00
  Monday in the institution's time zone. That is the first Monday 06:00 after
  the week closes.
- **S8, student variant.** `api/student.py:166` passes `claims.user_id` to
  `UUID()` with no guard, so a malformed claim raises a 500.

Read first: SPEC §3.1 and §5.2, ADR 0142 (the effective clock), and the student
refusal vocabulary in `backend/app/api/student.py`.

## Done when

1. **C1.** Clearing a judged comment on revise answers 422 with its copy. The
   form stays and shows the refusal inline (vitest), and a 409 still means
   "closed".
2. **C2.** The log text no longer says "not retried".
3. **C4.** Editing a set whose length no section runs shows that length
   selected, and an unchanged save sends it (vitest).
4. **C6.** A week is unavailable at 05:59 and published at 06:00 Monday in the
   institution's time zone, on the effective clock. `published_course_weeks`
   and `instructor_report` share one predicate, which ADR 0184 records. SPEC
   §3.1's "Monday morning" becomes "Monday 06:00".
5. **S8.** A malformed `user_id` claim on the survey read answers the student
   refusal, not a 500.

## Known traps

- **The calendar bomb.** E2E worlds that read a report straight after close
  now need the clock at Monday 06:00. Pin the clock; never rely on the real
  date.
- **Time zones.** 06:00 is in the institution's time zone, not UTC. Test a
  week that closes across a daylight-saving change.

- Moving the summary job out of `reporting.py`. Left out of this epic.
