## 2026-09-05 — the gates on `self.app` — did not work, and why

First build of `DevControlRoute` wrapped the ASGI application `Route.__init__`
builds, on the reasoning that wrapping there keeps Starlette's own handling of
synchronous and asynchronous endpoints intact. It reddened eight tests: `POST
/dev/passback` answered `303` on a production build, and answered `303` to a
cross-site `Origin` in development. Every gate was inert.

The cause is in `fastapi/routing.py::_IncludedRouter._build_effective_context`
on the pinned 0.141.1. `include_router` does not serve the route objects a
router holds. For a plain `starlette.routing.Route` it builds a **fresh** `Route`
from `route.endpoint`, `list(route.methods or [])`, `route.name` and
`route.include_in_schema`, and serves that. The subclass and anything it put on
`self.app` are discarded at dispatch, while the original object stays in
`router.routes` — which is why the CSRF sweep found the class and reported it
guarded over a route with no gate running at all. Exactly the shape
`docs/MISTAKES.md` entry 35 describes, one level out.

The endpoint is the only part of a route that survives that rebuild, so that is
where the wrapper went — which is what the work order (D4) said in the first
place: "its constructor wraps the endpoint". Recorded in ADR 0141 and in the
class's own docstring, because the next person to tidy this will reach for
`self.app` for the same reason.
