## 2026-09-13 — probed the composite foreign key onto a generated column first

The breakdown warned that `course.level` is a **stored generated** column and
that a composite foreign key onto it might be refused, in which case the answer
was to stop and report rather than improvise a trigger. Probed it against
Postgres 17 in a throwaway database (`e5_01_fkprobe`, created and dropped) with
the exact shape the ticket settles: a unique over `(id, level)` where `level` is
generated, a composite key referencing it, and a delete of the referenced row.
**Accepted, and `RESTRICT` fired.** So the settled mechanism stands and nothing
had to be invented.
