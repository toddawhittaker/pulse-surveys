# E5-10 — the report page joins the benchmark payload

## Attempt 1 — 2026-09-14, light lane

Branch `e5/report-benchmark-join`, cut from the epic head `b166ca4`.

### What was built

**The reconciliation landed in the component types, not in a mapper on the
page** (ADR 0171). `OverlaySeries`, `OverlayPoint` and the workload column types
are respelled as the wire spells them — `course_week`, a `mean` that is a whole
`{suppressed, reason, figure}`, and a workload column that is two independently
sealed figures — so the page passes `streams.<stream>.benchmark` and
`workload_benchmark` straight through. The wire types are written once in
`frontend/src/api/instructor.ts`; the components keep structurally identical
copies of their own, because `PulseTrendChart.test.tsx` refuses an import from
`../api` in the trend family and this ticket does not widen that guard.

**Suppression is read per week and per cell.** A trend week is drawable only if
its own seal says exactly `false` beside a real number; a series with no drawable
week takes E5-07's notice, and a series with some draws those and leaves a gap
at each withheld week. A workload cell is sealed on its own, which makes a column
that reports its median and withholds its mean expressible for the first time.

**The deferred panel crash is closed.** A member arriving without its `points`
array now yields no drawable week rather than `undefined` into the week-axis
spread. The deferred entry has a dated resolution paragraph rather than a
deletion.

### The divergence list (E4-11's precedent, criterion 3)

Four divergences between E5's payload sketch and the shipped schema, every one
resolved by moving the frontend to the schema:

1. a series-level `suppressed`/`reason` — does not exist on the wire;
2. a suppressed series carries `points: []` — on the wire every published week is
   a point and each point says whether it is suppressed;
3. a point's `mean` is a bare number — on the wire it is a whole sealed figure,
   and the number is at `mean.figure`;
4. a workload column is one flag over a bare `mean` and `median` — on the wire
   the two are sealed independently.

Swept by `scratchpad/sweep-e5-10.py`, three patterns each proven against a line
that certainly carries the divergence and a line that certainly does not before
the tree is read. Over `frontend/src` at the epic head it reports **46 hits in
three files**; over this branch, **0 hits, 71 files read**.

### What did not run, and why

**The two new end-to-end cases have never been executed**, and neither has the
rewritten assertion in `instructor-report.spec.ts`. They are written, they
typecheck, they lint and Playwright collects them; nothing has driven them
against a stack. Two things about the development stack stand in the way, and
both are decisions about shared infrastructure rather than about this diff:

  - **the running `mock-lms` image predates E5-12.** It has no
    `PRIOR_TERM_SECTIONS` and its launch page offers no `BIOL-310-U5FF`,
    `BIOL-310-U6WW`, `BIOL-310-R5FF` or `BIOL-215-E5WW`, so the prior term
    cannot be launched at all. The database agrees: it holds one term, `Fall
    2026`, and no `Spring 2026` row for `scripts/seed_benchmark_history.py` to
    find;
  - **the running `api` image serves a frontend built without this change**, so
    a drive against it would be a drive against the page E4 shipped.

Both are cleared by a rebuild of the stack's images from this worktree followed
by `alembic upgrade head` and `python scripts/seed.py`, which is the Docker gate
the orchestrator runs serially. This attempt did not run it: the brief reserves
it, and the database is shared with a build running in parallel.
