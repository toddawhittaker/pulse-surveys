**The criterion 2 ruling, recorded.** A course's level derives from its number

visible-removal path is the whole of what criterion 2 can exercise, and
"matching members survive" is provable only by re-choosing the same level,
which is what `ComparisonSetForm.test.tsx` does. The criterion is met as
written, and this note is here so that a later reader does not go looking for
the mixed-survival test that cannot exist.

**MED — criterion 2's unreachable middle, recorded.** A course's level derives
