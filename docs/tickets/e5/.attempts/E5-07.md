**One departure from the work order's settled decisions**, stated here and in
the stylesheet: the two comparison lines are `--spruce-60`, not the brief's
`--mist` and `--mist` at 50%. Mist is 2.58:1 against paper and about 1.6:1 at
half opacity, under SC 1.4.11's 3:1 for a graphical object that carries meaning,
and these two lines are the ticket's whole content. It is the correction the E4
boundary round already made for the hero line and the term-week sub-label,
arriving at the lines E5 adds; the hero's own colour is untouched, and the
brief's dashed-and-dotted distinction is what tells the two overlays apart. The
divergence between the shipped code and the brief is recorded in
`docs/tickets/e5/deferred.md` with Todd as its owner.

**One reading of a settled decision, rather than a departure.** The work order
says `OverlayPoint` mirrors `TrendPoint`'s field naming and "week-axis fields".
It carries `courseWeek` and `mean` and **no** `termWeek`, which is the payload
sketch's own shape: a benchmark is past-referencing (SPEC §5.1), so the figure
behind one point spans several terms and has no single term week to name, and a
column filled by counting from the section's would be the client derivation SPEC
§2.2 forbids.

**Two deferrals**, both in `docs/tickets/e5/deferred.md` in the same diff: the
brief's mist mapping (owner Todd), and this ticket's stroke pins living beside
its own tests rather than in `reportContrastTokens.test.ts` (owner E5-13, to
