## 2026-08-21 — the four routes, tests first (test-author, then verifier)

Unusual for this repository: **the guards are the whole of the change and no
production code moved.** The four routes in the scope list are properties of
`docker-compose.yml` and `docker-compose.override.yml` that the two files
already satisfy, so the work was building rules that can see them. Written by
the `test-author` agent against the ticket and the spec, and re-run
independently by `verifier` — 90 tests in the two modules, 435 in the whole unit
suite, all green.

What landed, all in `tests/unit/test_compose_stack.py` unless said otherwise:

- `ALLOWED_BIND_MOUNTS`, keyed by `(file, service)`, holding the four mounts the
  two files declare today (route 1).
- `named_volume_source`, resolving a service's named volumes through the
  top-level `volumes:` section and its `driver_opts`, and **refusing** a volume
  shape it cannot classify rather than reading it as harmless (route 2).
- The delivered-value comparison in `tests/unit/test_env_example_resolves.py`,
  with the delivered set computed from the Compose files rather than hand-listed
  (route 3).
- `normalised_bind_source`, applied to both sides of every comparison, with the
  alternate-spelling reachability measured against the local daemon on
  2026-08-21 by the orchestrating session and recorded in its docstring
  (route 4).

`bind_sources` gained the document and the project directory as parameters, so
the allowlist, the sensitive check and the privilege comparison against `api`
all consume the resolved, normalised set and none of them can be left behind.

points at it, because the docstring is where the five reviewer passes and the
