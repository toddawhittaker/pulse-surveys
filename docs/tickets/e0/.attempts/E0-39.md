## 2026-08-22 — layer 1 and layer 2 in `backend/app/config.py` — worked

Five `oidc_*` fields moved out of the defaulted "two entry doors' addresses"
block and into the required group, immediately after `environment`. Two
`field_validator`s at the foot of the class refuse the mock outside development:
one over the four URLs comparing `urlsplit(value).hostname` against
`MOCK_IDENTITY_PROVIDER_HOST`, one over `oidc_client_id` comparing the whole
value against `MOCK_IDENTITY_PROVIDER_CLIENT_ID`.

Two things decided while writing it:

- **Field validators rather than a model validator.** The tests require the
  refusal to *name the field*, and `_describe_invalid_settings` builds its lines
  out of the field name, the static `description=`, and pydantic's error code —
  it never uses the validator's own message. A model validator's error carries an
  empty `loc`, so it would render as "(unknown variable)" and both
  "names the field" tests would stay red. A field validator gets the name for
  free and keeps the module's existing idiom.
- **Reading `ENVIRONMENT` through `info.data`** makes declaration order
  load-bearing: pydantic validates in declaration order, so the five have to sit
  after `environment` or `info.data.get("environment")` is empty and nothing is
  ever refused. Written down in a comment at the block rather than left implicit.
  `_is_a_deployment` treats a non-string as *not* a deployment, which is safe
  because `environment` is itself required — a `Settings` that reaches the
  validator with no environment is already being refused for that variable.
