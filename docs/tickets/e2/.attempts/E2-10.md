### The three decisions that needed recording

**ADR 0116 — self-host the faces.** E1-04's deferred item 1 was due against this
screen and is closed in place in `docs/tickets/e1/deferred.md`. The alternative —
a `<link>` to a font host — is a third-party request made on a student's behalf
from inside their institution's iframe, which is E0-18's own reasoning
transferred. Measured on the branch: the payload the bundle gate counts is
**93,678 bytes gzipped with the faces and 93,459 without**, so the six
`@font-face` rules cost **219 bytes**; the faces themselves are **119,100 bytes
of woff2** the gate does not count, plus **147,028 bytes of woff** that ships
because each `@fontsource` stylesheet lists both and no supported browser fetches
it. The pre-ticket baseline at `62323c3` measured **85,487 entry / 87,878
total**, so the whole ticket is **+4,324 entry / +5,800 total**, of which the
webfont decision is 219.

**ADR 0117 — two `fetch` calls rather than a generated client.** SPEC §13's tree
draws "generated client from backend OpenAPI + TanStack Query hooks" and E1-04
left the choice to this screen in as many words. Two calls do not pay for a
generator, its output, a build step and a currency check, and the generated
client would still need the outcome mapping — which is the interesting half,
because what a status means here is which of `app.copy`'s sentences it carries.

**The copy split.** Question wording, the Likert bounds, the slider's range and
step, and §3.2's conditional rule all come off the question rows and are consumed
as they arrive; SPEC §3.2 quotes no wording for the two comments or the slider,

### The finding, and why it was right

A 401 from `GET /student/survey` rendered the calm empty-week state — "There is
no survey open for you yet." The launch session lives an hour and a survey window
stands open from Friday evening to Sunday night, so the ordinary way to meet that
401 is a student coming back to yesterday's tab **inside a window that is still
open**, and what they were told was that nothing was due. The submit path never
made the mistake; it maps its own 401 to a refusal. Only the read path collapsed
it.

The uncomfortable part is that this sitting's own comment argued for the
behaviour: "nobody is *sent* here without a session … the calm 'nothing open for
you yet' is the honest answer." That reasoning is about somebody who never had a
session and it silently covered somebody whose session ran out, which is the
larger population by far. The comment was written to keep
`landing-views.spec.ts` green without changing it, and that is the tell — a
justification shaped by a test rather than by the person reading the screen.

### The fix

`SurveyRead`'s 401 outcome is `session-ended` rather than `no-session`, the
screen gives it a state of its own, and two new entries in
`frontend/src/copy/studentSurvey.ts` say what it says: **"Open this from your
course"** over "This page is not signed in, so it cannot say what is due. Open
Pulse Surveys from inside your course in the LMS, and this week's questions will
be here." It refuses the claim the finding is about in as many words, and it is
calm and blames nobody — a session running out is not something a student did.

`app.copy.student_read`'s `student.not_a_student` is the server's sentence for
the same refusal and is deliberately not what renders. That one answers a
request; this one has to tell the person looking at a rendered page what to do
next in it, and it has to say so when the 401 carried no body at all — which is
what a proxy in front of the tool answers with.

Only the read fetch's 401 is mapped. Every other outcome is untouched.

Three comments that justified the old behaviour were rewritten rather than
deleted: the screen's module docstring, the mapping in the effect, and
`SurveyRead`'s own documentation.

### The other ticket's spec that had to change, and why

`tests/e2e/landing-views.spec.ts` navigates to `/app/student` **carrying no
session**, so it read the 401 state — and it asserted the calm sentence. The
assertion was correct for the landing view E1-04 shipped and is false for the
survey that replaced it, so the student row's sentence is now the refusal's. The
heading is unchanged, the testid is unchanged, the other four rows are untouched,
and the reason is written into the row and the file header. Nothing was skipped,
weakened or deleted; one expectation was corrected to the behaviour the review
asked for, and the case still fails against the defect (below).

### Proved, both readers, against the shape the review found

A sixth case in `tests/e2e/student-survey.spec.ts`: launch for real, assert the
form is on screen — the control that says this student, section and clock would
otherwise show a form — then write a token the server will not verify into
`sessionStorage`, reload, and require the refusal state, the *absence* of the
calm sentence, no form, no section block, and no word of error vocabulary.

The mutation is the defect itself, put back: mapping the 401 to
`{kind: 'sections', sections: []}`. It reds the new case **and** the updated
landing-views case, and leaves the other nine green. Two independent readers
hold the fix, and both were seen failing against it.
