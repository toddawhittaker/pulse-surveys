Two things worth knowing next time:

- **`REPORT_STREAMS` lives in `survey.py`, not in `report.py`.** Both
  `question.stream` and `weekly_summary.stream` enumerate the same two tokens, so
  one of the two modules has to own them; putting them beside the column that is
  the *source* of the fact means the new module depends on the old one rather
  than the other way round. `scripts/seed.py` unpacks the same tuple.
- **The backfill is by kind first and by ordinal second**, in three `CASE` limbs
  where two produce the same token. The `ELSE` is a separate decision (a
  placeholder for rows outside §3.2's shape) and collapsing it into the `COURSE`
  limb would make one rule out of two.

- **The fallback is not the cause.** Running tests 1 and 3 only (so the failing

  green against the crippled backfill, and all three of the older module's tests
  went green too. That is `docs/MISTAKES.md` entry 3, and it is also the
  backfill-plus-preserve shape `3f6907349751`'s docstring records E2-16
  measuring and deleting. Reverted, and the file verified byte-identical to the
  committed one afterwards.

The objection's proposed repair is one function behind the wall:
