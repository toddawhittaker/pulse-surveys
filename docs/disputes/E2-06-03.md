# E2-06-03 — The privilege record is a closed inventory, and `survey_window`'s two verbs are not in it

**Ticket:** E2-06
**Test:** `tests/integration/test_identity_grants.py::test_the_runtime_roles_hold_no_privilege_on_a_base_table_beyond_the_reveals_own`

## The test

It reads, out of the catalog, every privilege `pulse_app` and `pulse_care` hold on
every base table in `public`, and compares that set against a hand-written
constant **as an equality in both directions**:

```python
RUNTIME_BASE_TABLE_PRIVILEGES = frozenset(
    {
        (CARE_ROLE, "role_assignment", "SELECT"),
        (APPLICATION_ROLE, "classification", "SELECT"),
        (APPLICATION_ROLE, "classification", "INSERT"),
        …
        (APPLICATION_ROLE, "clock_override", "SELECT"),
        (APPLICATION_ROLE, "clock_override", "INSERT"),
        (APPLICATION_ROLE, "clock_override", "DELETE"),
    }
)
```

Its own docstring says why the shape is an equality rather than a ceiling:

> **What the equality buys beyond a ceiling.** Two of the three are `pulse_app`
> on `classification`, and the interesting half of that entry is what is *not*
> in it. SPEC §8 requires `classification` to be append-only; `SELECT, INSERT`
> granted with `UPDATE`, `DELETE` and `TRUNCATE` withheld is what makes
> append-only a property of the database rather than a rule the next writer has
> to remember. Nothing else in this suite asserts that, and an equality is the
> only shape that can: `>=` would be satisfied by a connection that can rewrite
> a moderation verdict.

The constant is `invariant`-marked with its test, so it is also in the isolated
§4.1 pass.

## What I believe it asserts incorrectly

Nothing. **The test is right and the shape is right**; it is a closed inventory,
and this is the widening it exists to make somebody argue for out loud. E2-06
grants the application role two verbs on a base table the constant does not name,
so it goes red until the constant gains exactly:

```python
        (APPLICATION_ROLE, "survey_window", "SELECT"),
        (APPLICATION_ROLE, "survey_window", "INSERT"),
```

and nothing else. That edit is in `tests/`, which this lane's implementer may not
make.

## The spec text I am relying on

SPEC §3.1, for what the grant is spent on:

> - Default rhythm (institution configuration): **opens Friday 18:00, closes
>   Sunday 23:59:59, reports available after window close Monday morning** in the
>   institution timezone (default `America/New_York`).
> - A section's active weeks derive from its section code and the term calendar
>   (§2.2).
> - Students see exactly one open survey at a time per section. Missed weeks
>   cannot be back-filled (this keeps the signal weekly and the grading
>   unambiguous).

The last sentence is the one that decides the *withheld* verbs, and it is why the
pair is `SELECT, INSERT` rather than anything wider. A missed week cannot be
back-filled and a closed window is closed; with no `UPDATE` the application role
structurally cannot move a `closes_at`, and with no `DELETE` it cannot remove a
window a response or a participation denominator was counted against.

ADR 0009 is the standing rule the constant enforces — the application role holds
the narrowest set of privileges that lets the product work — and ADR 0111 records
this ticket's decisions, including the grant and its two withheld verbs.

## What I tried

Not "tried and failed" — measured, in both directions, before asking for the
widening at all.

1. **Is the grant needed?** Without it, eight tests across the three development
   console modules fail with
   `psycopg.errors.InsufficientPrivilege: permission denied for table survey_window`
   behind a 500. One of the eight is `invariant`-marked, so
   `pytest -m invariant` and `scripts/ci/check_invariants.py` are red too.
2. **Is it sufficient, and are both verbs load-bearing?** Applying exactly
   `GRANT SELECT, INSERT ON public.survey_window TO pulse_app` from a pytest
   plugin outside `tests/`, changing nothing else, turned those three modules
   green: 13 passed. `SELECT` is the console's read and the derivation's "which
   windows does this section already have"; `INSERT` is the derivation writing
   one. Neither is spare.
3. **Is the withheld half real?** After the migration, the catalog reports exactly
   `['INSERT', 'SELECT']` for `pulse_app` on `survey_window`; after
   `alembic downgrade -1`, `[]`; after upgrading again, `['INSERT', 'SELECT']`.
   No `UPDATE`, no `DELETE`, no `TRUNCATE`.

The alternative I did not take, and would refuse if it were suggested: putting
the verbs on a one-table view instead. A one-table view is auto-updatable in
Postgres and views are outside this constant's comparison, so it would go green
while granting the same reach — a view built for no reason but to sit outside a
test. The same docstring calls a grant on the table behind a view "SPEC §8's
separation undone".

## Why I believe the test rather than my code is at fault

Neither is at fault. This is the third case of a shape this project has already
ruled on twice: a ticket that legitimately needs a new base-table privilege reds
a deliberately closed inventory whose record lives behind the test wall.
`docs/disputes/E2-04-02.md` is the precedent, for `clock_override`'s three verbs,
and that constant's own comment predicted this one — "E2 will do it again when the
first student write path needs a grant on `response`". E2-06 is that ticket one
table early: the console reads windows and the worker writes them.

The coordinator has ruled on this in advance, and the ruling is recorded here so
the record carries it rather than a session's memory: **the two tuples above are
approved, the change is the test author's, and it is exactly two tuples — no
third verb, no widening to a pattern, and no relaxation of the equality to a
superset check.**
