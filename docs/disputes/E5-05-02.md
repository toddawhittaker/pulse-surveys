# E5-05-02 — The university-line control is false for the course panel: the hero answers no course rating outside course week 1

**Ticket:** E5-05
**Test:** `tests/integration/test_the_benchmark_payload_plants_both_sides_of_both_minimums.py::test_the_week_one_respondent_short_is_suppressed_beside_its_passing_twin[COURSE]`
and `::test_the_week_one_section_short_is_suppressed_while_the_university_line_is_shown[COURSE]`

The `[INSTRUCTOR]` half of each of those two tests passes. Only the course-stream
parametrization is disputed, and only its control assertion.

## The test

From the respondent-minimum test:

```python
    assert WEEK_THIN_PEOPLE in university and numbers_of(university[WEEK_THIN_PEOPLE]), (
        f"The control failed before the assertion it protects: the university line at course week "
        f"{WEEK_THIN_PEOPLE} — the same people plus the hero's own respondents, which is above "
        f"`{report_api_contract.minimum_respondents}` of "
        f"{minimums[report_api_contract.minimum_respondents]} — carries no figure: "
        f"{university.get(WEEK_THIN_PEOPLE)!r}."
    )
```

and from the section-minimum test:

```python
    assert WEEK_THIN_SECTIONS in university and numbers_of(university[WEEK_THIN_SECTIONS]), (
        f"The control failed before the assertion it protects: at course week "
        f"{WEEK_THIN_SECTIONS} the university line covers the set's {sections - 1} sections plus "
        f"the hero's own, which is exactly `{report_api_contract.minimum_sections}` of {sections}, "
        f"and it carries no figure: {university.get(WEEK_THIN_SECTIONS)!r}. Until it does, the "
        "suppression below is satisfied by a payload with no university line at all."
    )
```

Both stand on the module docstring's premise:

> **Every suppression sits beside a figure that arrived.** In each test the
> control is asserted first and in the same payload: the passing weeks of the
> same series, or the university line over the same rows plus the hero — which
> E5's breakdown decision 5 keeps in the population the comparison set excludes.

## What I believe it asserts incorrectly

"The same rows plus the hero" is true of the world in the *workload* currency and
in the *instructor-rating* currency. It is false in the *course-rating* currency,
because the hero section has no course-rating rows in any of the four weeks this
ticket's world plants into.

`tests/fixtures/report_api.py` seeds the hero's answers like this, in the loop
over `ANSWERED_BY`:

```python
            if course_week == FULL_WEEK:
                comments[INSTRUCTOR_STREAM] = FULL_WEEK_COMMENTS[place]
                ratings = {
                    RATING_POSITION[COURSE_STREAM]: FULL_WEEK_COURSE_RATINGS[place],
                    WORKLOAD_POSITION: FULL_WEEK_WORKLOAD_HOURS[place],
                }
                ...
            elif course_week in HELD_COMMENTS:
                comments[INSTRUCTOR_STREAM] = HELD_COMMENTS[course_week][place]
                ratings = {RATING_POSITION[INSTRUCTOR_STREAM]: 3}
            else:
                ratings = {RATING_POSITION[INSTRUCTOR_STREAM]: 4}
```

`FULL_WEEK = 1`, and the benchmark world plants into course weeks 2, 3, 4 and 5.
So in every week this module reads, the hero's respondents answer the instructor
rating and nothing else: no course rating, and therefore no course-stream
respondent and no course-stream section from the hero.

E5-04 seals each figure against **its own contributors** — the distinct people who
answered *that stream* and the distinct sections those answers came from. So the
university course-rating line at a benchmark week is computed over the comparison
set's rows alone:

  - course week 3 (one person short): 14 people, which is below the configured
    respondent minimum of 15, so the figure is suppressed;
  - course week 4 (one section short): 2 sections, below the configured section
    minimum of 3, so the figure is suppressed.

That is what the route serves, and both controls fail on it:

```
The control failed before the assertion it protects: the university line at course week 3
— the same people plus the hero's own respondents, which is above
`benchmark_min_respondents_default` of 15 — carries no figure:
{'suppressed': True, 'reason': 'below-minimum', 'figure': None}.
```

```
The control failed before the assertion it protects: at course week 4 the university line
covers the set's 2 sections plus the hero's own, which is exactly
`benchmark_min_sections_default` of 3, and it carries no figure:
{'suppressed': True, 'reason': 'below-minimum', 'figure': None}.
```

The same payload shows the instructor-stream university line at both weeks, which
is the difference the fixture creates.

## The spec text I am relying on

SPEC §4.1, item 7:

> No figure computed from a comparison set is shown below the benchmark minimum
> — a mean, a median, or any other statistic, not only a drawn line. A
> comparison figure over fewer than the configured number of sections is
> suppressed exactly as a line is (§5.1).

SPEC §5.1, on what the university line is:

> the university-wide line is all same-length+level sections institution-wide

Both sentences are about the figure's own population. The only implementation
that would show the course-stream university line at course weeks 3 and 4 of this
world is one that seals a rating mean with counts that are not the rating's own —
the week's overall respondents and sections, which include people who answered
only the instructor question. That is the defect E5-04's own security round
removed, recorded in `app/services/benchmarks.py`:

> the first version sealed a per-stream rating mean with the week's overall
> counts and a workload mean with counts of people who had reported no hours,
> both of which are larger than the figure's own population and so both of which
> show figures §4.1 item 7 means to withhold (`docs/MISTAKES.md` entry 50's
> class).

## What I tried

1. **Read the counts back rather than reasoning about them.** The failure
   messages above are the route's own answer, and the instructor-stream halves of
   the same two tests pass over the same payload — so this is not an empty
   series, a missing member or an unpopulated university population.
2. **Checked where the difference comes from**, in the fixture rather than by
   guessing: the `ANSWERED_BY` loop quoted above writes a course rating only in
   `FULL_WEEK`, which is course week 1, and this ticket's four weeks are 2 to 5.
3. **Considered whether the assembly could make the control true.** It could
   only by asking `benchmark_trend` for the university population with a
   different stream, or by sealing the course-rating mean with the instructor
   stream's counts. The first serves the wrong panel's numbers; the second is the
   disclosure E5-04 removed. Neither is a candidate.
4. I did not change `tests/fixtures/report_benchmarks.py` or
   `tests/fixtures/report_api.py` to plant the hero's missing course ratings —
   the test wall is the lane's point, and E4-07's world is another ticket's
   premise, which the fixture module says it deliberately leaves alone ("The
   hero's own answers stay exactly what E4-07's world planted").

## Why I believe the test rather than my code is at fault

The control asserts a property of the *world*, not of this ticket's code, and the
world does not have it for the course stream. Every other assertion in both tests
passes: the comparison line is suppressed at the right week for the right
minimum, the passing twins carry their own means, and the point stays in the
series. What fails is a sentence about the hero's contribution that is true for
one of the two panels.

Two repairs are open to the test author, and they are not equivalent:

  - **Plant the hero's course-stream ratings in the benchmark weeks**, inside
    `plant_the_benchmark_cohort` rather than in E4-07's fixture, so that the
    university line really is "the same rows plus the hero" in both currencies.
    This keeps the control as sharp as it was meant to be — one section and a
    handful of people apart from the comparison line — and it is the repair I
    would choose.
  - **Or scope the control to the currency the world has**: assert the
    university-line control on the instructor panel, and for the course panel use
    the passing weeks of the same university series as the control instead.
    Cheaper, and weaker: it stops proving that the two populations differ on the
    course panel at all.

I have implemented everything else in this ticket and left these two
parametrizations red rather than working around them.
