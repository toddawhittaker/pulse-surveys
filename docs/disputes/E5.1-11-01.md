# E5.1-11-01 — E5.1-02's grant-ending door test walks a section the day after it ended

**Ticket:** E5.1-11
**Test:** `tests/integration/test_a_complete_roster_walk_ends_the_teaching_grant_it_dropped.py::test_a_complete_walk_that_stops_listing_her_as_instructor_shuts_her_out_of_the_section` (both parameters, `left-the-roster` and `lost-the-instructor-role`)

It fails at the `not in listed` assertion, line 278, in both parameters.

## What I believe it asserts incorrectly

It asserts that a complete walk ends the instructor's grant, and it walks a
section whose last day has already passed. The `report_door` world starts the
development clock at `AFTER_THE_LAST_WINDOW` (`tests/fixtures/report_api.py`,
line 479), after every one of the section's six windows has closed, so every
course week is published. Measured inside the sync with a temporary print in
`_ingest` (removed): **today = 2026-11-09, `section.end_date` = 2026-11-08**,
for both parameters.

E5.1-11's settled decision is that no teaching grant is ended once the
section has ended: when `today > section.end_date`, the grant-ending pass does
nothing. So on this world the correct behaviour is that her grant survives,
and the test demands the opposite.

The past-term state is incidental to what the test means to prove. It was
written for E5.1-02 (commit 9ed54929), before the past-term rule existed, and
it borrowed the report door's world because that world gives a readable report
to assert against. Nothing in its docstring is about a past term.

## The spec text I am relying on

The ticket, `docs/tickets/e5.1/E5.1-11-roster-roles-and-term-end.md`,
"Decisions (settled)":

> **No teaching grant is ended once the section has ended**: when `today` is
> after `section.end_date` (inclusive last day), the grant-ending pass does
> nothing. On `end_date` itself it still runs. Closing enrollments is
> unchanged.

and its Context, item 2:

> The hourly sync walks every section with a stored roster address forever.
> Platforms commonly end or inactivate teacher enrollments when a course
> concludes. The next complete walk then ends the instructor's teaching grant
> on their own past-term section, so they lose their past reports, and SPEC
> §5's seal changes what it treats as "sections she teaches".

That is exactly this test's scenario, with the opposite expectation.

SPEC §2.1 ("LMS-owned (read-only in Pulse; hourly roster sync + launch-time
ingestion): courses, sections, section codes, enrollments, teaching
instructors.") does not say how long after a term the roster keeps that
authority; the ticket settles it.

## What I tried

One approach: the gate `today > section.end_date` in `_ingest`, before
`_end_unsupported_teaching_grants`, reading `today` from the clock service as
the sync already does. It turns all 19 tests in the two new E5.1-11 files
green, including the boundary pair (`end_date + 1` keeps the grant,
`end_date` ends it). It reds these two parameters and nothing else among the
1,567 tests in every file matching `roster` under `tests/integration` and
`tests/unit`.

No reading of the gate satisfies both. Gating on the term's `end_date`, or on
`end_date + 1`, would make the new boundary pair red, and the new file pins
those near-misses on purpose.

## Why I believe the test rather than my code is at fault

The two tests cannot both hold: the new criterion 2 test (`…the_day_after_the_section_ends_leaves_the_dropped_instructors_grant`)
says a walk on `end_date + 1` keeps the grant, and this test walks on
`end_date + 1` and requires it gone. The ticket's Decisions are settled and
name the newer rule. The old test's intent, that a complete walk which stops
listing her shuts her out at the door, still holds for a current section; its
world simply needs a section that has not yet ended on the pretended day.

A fix that keeps its intent: in this test (and its siblings in the same file
that build the same world, if any begin to depend on it), move the walked
section's `end_date` on or after the pretended today before walking, or
pretend a day inside the section's span while still after the week whose
report it reads. The test author owns that choice.

I have left the behaviour as the ticket settles it and not altered the gate
to pass this test.
